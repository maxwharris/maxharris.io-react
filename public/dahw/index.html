DAHW Digital Audio Handheld Workstation

A music studio for iPhone. Sample any sound, even the audio from a video, warp it to your song, chop it onto pads, then arrange, mix and export the track.

Beta on TestFlight (invite only) Coming to the App Store, free.

No account. Your music stays on your phone.

Designed with experience

I’m a beat maker, and I wanted a mobile setup I could rely on. When I hear a sound, I want to catch it, chop it and build the beat right there, without waiting to get back to my desk. DAHW gives every screen one job and big controls for your thumb, in the words you know from a desktop DAW: tracks, clips, devices, Warp, sends and returns, C3 as MIDI note 60. Nothing leaves your iPhone unless you export it.

What’s in it

Tap one to see what it does and how to use it.

  • Sampler Record, import, or take the sound of a video
  • Pads Play slices and drums, with note repeat
  • Drum Rack Sixteen pads, each a full Sampler
  • Arrangement Tracks, clips, the loop and recording
  • Piano roll Draw notes, chords and melodies
  • Analog and FM Two synths, with presets
  • Effects and mixer Eight effects, sends and returns
  • Export The whole song, or its stems
  • Light and dark Light by default, dark for dim rooms
