DAHW Digital Audio Handheld Workstation

A music studio for iPhone. Sample any sound, even the audio from a video, warp it to your song, chop it onto pads, then arrange, mix and export the track.

Beta on TestFlight (invite only) Coming to the App Store, free.

No account. Your music stays on your phone.

Why I made it

I make music in a desktop DAW, and sampling is the part I do most: catch a sound, find its tempo, loop it, chop it, play it. I wanted that on my phone without squeezing a whole studio onto a small screen. So every screen in DAHW has one job and big controls you can work with your thumb, and it uses the words you already know: tracks, clips, devices, Warp, sends and returns, C3 as MIDI note 60. There is no account, and nothing leaves your iPhone unless you export it.

Max Harris

What’s in it

  • Sampler

    Record with the mic, import any audio file, or take the sound from a video. Set markers and the loop, zoom in to a single cycle, detect the BPM, warp it in Re-Pitch or Complex, transpose it, slice it, and play it on a chromatic keyboard.

  • Pads

    Play slices or drums on a full-screen grid of 16 pads (8 by 2 in landscape), with four banks, velocity from where you hit, and note repeat from 1/4 to 1/32, triplets included.

  • Drum Rack

    Sixteen pads, and every pad is a full Sampler. Deal a random kit, re-roll one pad or one kind of sound, load your own samples, save the racks you like, and program beats with velocity and swing.

  • Arrangement

    MIDI and audio tracks, clips you trim, split, duplicate, loop and consolidate, a loop brace on the ruler, and recording with a count-in. Flatten any clip to audio, and undo every edit.

  • Piano roll

    Tap to draw a note and drag to stretch it, select and move notes, shape velocity, quantize, and transpose by a step or an octave, in portrait or landscape.

  • Analog and FM

    Analog has two oscillators, unison, a 12 or 24 dB filter, two envelopes and an LFO. FM has four operators and 11 algorithms, each drawn as a diagram. Both come with presets, and you can save your own.

  • Effects and mixer

    Eight effects on any track, return or the master: EQ Three, Auto Filter, Compressor, Saturator, Chorus, Delay, Reverb and Limiter. Two sends, two returns, and a mixer with delay compensation, so every track stays in time.

  • Export

    Mix the song down to a 24-bit WAV or an M4A, or export stems: a WAV for every track and return, all the same length, so they line up in any DAW.

  • Light and dark

    Light by default, with a dark mode for dim rooms, or let it follow your iPhone. Every screen is drawn for both.

Getting started

DAHW opens on a demo song, Late Night Flip, so there is something to play right away.

Five tabs run along the bottom: Arrange, Clip, Device, Mix and Browse. Select a track or a clip in Arrange, and Clip and Device show it.

Get a sound into the Sampler

  1. Select a Sampler track and open Device. For a new one, tap Add in Arrange and choose Sampler.
  2. Tap More, then Load Sample… (Replace Sample… once there is one). Choose Files, Photos & Videos to use the sound of a video, or Recordings.
  3. To record, tap More, then Record… (or Record Over…), and press Record.
  4. DAHW detects the tempo as the sound loads. You can also start from Browse: pick a file, a video or a recording, then where it goes.

Set the loop and warp it

  1. On the Loop page, drag the Loop Start and Loop End handles under the waveform, or the bar between them to move the loop. S and E set where the sound starts and ends.
  2. Pinch to zoom, down to a single cycle. A double tap jumps between the whole sample and the loop.
  3. On Tempo, tap Detect or Tap, or set Loop Is to the number of bars the loop should be.
  4. On Pitch, set Warp to Re-Pitch (speed and pitch together, like tape) or Complex (tempo and pitch apart). The sample now follows the song’s tempo. Transpose and Detune sit beside it, and Render bakes it all into a new sample.

Play it on keys and pads

  1. Play the keyboard at the bottom of the Sampler. The diamond marks the root key (C3 at first), and the arrows beside the keys change octave.
  2. For pads, tap Classic at the top right and choose Slice. The Slices page slices by Transients, Beats or Manual, and the keyboard turns into slice pads.
  3. Tap Pads beside them for the full-screen pad view: banks A to D, Fixed or Touch velocity, and Repeat from 1/4 to 1/32 (T for triplets). Keys goes back.
  4. With Repeat off, long-press a pad to edit its slice: start, end, tune, gain, reverse and choke.

Build a beat with the Drum Rack

  1. In Arrange, tap Add and choose Drums. The new track starts with a random kit, ready to play.
  2. Open Device and tap the pads. The Kit page deals a New Random Rack or re-rolls every pad or one kind of sound, and Save This Rack… keeps one you like.
  3. On Steps, make a New Clip if there is none, then tap steps to program the selected pad, with Step Velocity and Swing.
  4. On Pad, Replace loads your own sound from Files, a video or Browse, Record records one, and Sampler opens the pad’s own Sampler.

Draw notes in the piano roll

  1. In Arrange, tap an empty part of a MIDI track’s lane to make a one-bar clip (drag its end to make it longer), then open Clip.
  2. In Draw mode , tap the grid to add a note and drag right to stretch it. Tap a note to delete it.
  3. In Select mode , tap notes or drag across the grid to select them, then drag them where they go.
  4. The Velocity, Loop and Transform pages set velocity, the clip’s loop, quantize and transpose. Fold shows only the notes in use.

Arrange clips and the loop

  1. Tap a clip to select it, drag it to move it, and drag its edges to trim it. The bar at the bottom has Split, Duplicate, Loop, Open and More.
  2. Long-press a clip for Rename, Color, Consolidate, Reverse, Open in Sampler, Flatten to Audio and the rest.
  3. The loop is the bar along the top of the ruler: drag its ends or its middle, or long-press it for Double Loop, Halve Loop and Loop to Selected Clip. Turn it on and off with Loop in the transport; with it off, drag across the ruler to draw a new one.
  4. Pinch to zoom, and scroll with two fingers.

Record

  1. Arm a track with the round Arm button beside M and S in its header. With nothing armed, Record arms the selected track.
  2. Press the red Record in the transport. DAHW counts in, then records from the playhead: the mic on an audio track, the keys or pads on a MIDI track.
  3. Press Record again to punch out and keep playing, or Stop to end. With the loop on, every pass is a take, and Undo removes the newest.
  4. Tap the position readout in the transport for the count-in, the metronome and Input Quantize.

Add effects and sends

  1. On Device, tap the device’s name at the top (Sampler, for example) to open the Device Chain, then tap Add Effect.
  2. Pick EQ Three, Auto Filter, Compressor, Saturator, Chorus, Delay, Reverb or Limiter. Drag the handle to reorder, and use the switch to turn one off.
  3. In Mix, the Sends page sends a track to Return A (a Reverb) and Return B (a Delay), before or after the fader.
  4. Chain on a mixer strip opens that strip’s effects, the returns and the master included.

Export a song or stems

  1. Tap the project’s name at the top left of Arrange, then Export.
  2. Mixdown makes one file of the whole song: choose WAV (24-bit) or M4A. Stems makes a WAV for every track and return; switch off the ones you don’t need. Sample exports one of the project’s samples.
  3. DAHW renders on your iPhone, from the first clip to the end of the last reverb and delay tails, then the share sheet opens: save to Files, AirDrop, or send it anywhere.

Support and privacy

Support

Found a problem or have a question? Email maxwilliamharris@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model, your iOS version and the DAHW version (shown in Settings), and what you were doing when it happened.

Testing through TestFlight? You can also send feedback with a screenshot from inside the TestFlight app.

Your projects

Your projects live only on your iPhone. To keep a copy of a song, export it (whole song or stems) from the Arrangement’s Export sheet and save it to Files.

What it runs on

iPhone with iOS 18 or later. On an iPad, the iPhone version runs.

Privacy

No account. Your music stays on your phone.

  • DAHW collects no data: no analytics, no ads, no tracking, and nothing is sent to a server.
  • The microphone is used only when you record.
  • Photos and Files hand DAHW only the video or file you pick.
