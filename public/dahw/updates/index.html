Updates

What changed in each build of DAHW, newest first.

TestFlight lists these builds as version 0.42 with the build number, for example 0.42 (45), while the app shows the same build as DAHW v0.45 on its Projects screen.

DAHW v0.54

October 22, 2026 Build 54

Sound and mix

  • New: Gyrosynth: play a track’s instrument by moving the phone. On an Analog, FM, Sampler or Drums device page, open More and pick Gyrosynth. The first time, hold the phone out in front of you and tap: that way becomes the middle.
  • Turn left and right to point at a note of the song key, low on the left, with a light tick at each new note. Thumb Hold, on at first, plays the note while your thumb is on the bottom of the screen; with it off, a flick of the phone plays it. A harder move plays louder, and twisting your wrist makes the sound brighter or darker.
  • On a Drums track it puts four drums around you, the snare straight ahead: point at one and flick to hit it. Record takes what you play as notes into a clip, with the take strip and Undo Take as in the pad view. The gear sets Range (1 to 3 octaves), Twist and Thumb Hold for each track.
  • New: Key Detect, an effect that tells you the key of a track, a return, or the whole song on the Master. Add it from the Device Chain, open it and play: it listens only while its page is open and passes the sound through untouched.
  • After a few seconds it shows the key, how sure it is and how far from A440 it sits, with its relative and the next best key to pick if it sounds wrong. Drums alone say No clear key. Use as Song Key makes it the song’s key, with Undo, and the key it found stays with the effect.

Everywhere

  • DAHW now plays during a phone call, or a video or voice call in another app: the song, keys, pads, previews and the metronome play, a little quieter under the call. Before, nothing played until the call ended.
  • Recording from the microphone is off during a call, and Record says so; MIDI tracks still record. Recording comes back by itself after the call (after Stop, if the song is playing).

DAHW v0.53

October 21, 2026 Build 53

Sampler

  • New: DAHW finds the key of every sample you bring in, as it finds the tempo. On the Pitch page, Transpose names it (A minor), or the key it plays in once you transpose it (in C minor). A single note, like an 808, shows its note instead (G1 −12 ct).
  • New: Key, at the top of the open Transpose: how sure it is, how far from A440 the sample sits, its relative and the next best key, and Detect to look again. To Song Key moves the sample into the song key and in tune in one step; Use as Song Key makes its key the song’s.
  • When the song has no key yet, the first sample with a clear key sets it, with a notice and Undo. A sample is never transposed or retuned by itself.
  • Dragging Transpose holds at each note of the song key, and dragging Detune holds where the sample is in tune with A440. For a single note, Set Root makes every key on the keyboard play it in tune.
  • New: A tuner. Open Record and, before a take, the card shows the note you play, how many cents off it is and which way to turn, then In tune with a check. It works for a guitar, a bass, keys or a voice, at A440, with nothing to set.
  • The first page tab shows a small down arrow after Classic, One-Shot or Slice: hold it to pick the play mode.

Arrangement and clips

  • The piano roll’s Scale now starts at the song key and follows it.

Drums

  • On a drum pad’s Pitch page, Transpose shows the hit’s note, and To Song Key tunes it to the nearest note of the song key.

Sound and mix

  • New: The song has a key. Tap the transport’s readout: Key sits under Tap Tempo and Signature. The Sampler, the piano roll and Vocal Tune follow it.
  • New: Vocal Tune, an effect that pulls a voice onto the song key or a key you pick. Speed sets how fast notes are pulled in (at 0, Hard, every note snaps for the robot sound). Flex lets slides and notes far from the key pass as sung, Vibrato makes the singer’s own vibrato smaller or bigger, and Humanize corrects long notes more gently than short ones.
  • Its picture shows what you sang and what plays. You hear it when the song plays and in exports, not yet while you record.

Projects and files

  • Settings > Storage now counts each sample once, so the total no longer grows as your projects join Samples.

Everywhere

  • Every hold is a little quicker: the Device tab, the Sampler’s first page tab, Play (back to the start), clips, track headers and the loop on the ruler respond about a third sooner. A quick tap is still a tap.

DAHW v0.52

October 20, 2026, evening Build 52

Sampler

  • New: The play mode moved to the Sampler’s first page tab, where Loop was. It reads Classic, One-Shot or Slice: tap it to open its page, or hold it for a moment to pick another mode (in Slice mode, also Trigger or Gate).
  • In Slice mode, Pad View now sits in the Sampler’s header, and in the same place in the pad view to take you back. The Pads button beside the slice pads is gone, so the pads get its room.
  • New: Edit, next to Pad View in the pad view. While it’s on, tapping a pad opens its editor instead of playing it. It replaces holding a pad to edit it.
  • New: An empty Sampler offers two ways in: New Sample records, or imports from Files or a video, and From Samples lists everything you’ve loaded, in any project, to search, preview and load.
  • New: Saved setups. When you Fit a sample (or Tap, type a Sample BPM, Set Bar 1 Here or Detect), its tempo, bar 1 and fitted loop are saved with it in Samples.
  • Load that sample again, in any project, and it comes in on bar 1, looped on its bars, with Warp on. The “Loaded in time” notice has Undo.
  • A sample with nothing saved yet opens on the Tempo page, with a short hint.
  • Each project keeps its own copy and settings. When Samples has a different setup for a sample, that project’s Tempo page offers Use Saved Setup.
  • Loop Is on the Tempo page is now saved with the project.

Arrangement and clips

  • New: Stretch an audio clip. Select it and drag the square grip on its bottom-right corner: the clip gets longer or shorter on the grid and its audio stretches to fill it. Warp turns on if it was off. The handle on the right edge above the corner still trims and extends.
  • With VoiceOver, a selected audio clip has Stretch Longer and Stretch Shorter, a grid step each.
  • On the Clip page, tap the header (the track’s icon and the clip’s name) to open the track’s device.
  • Where a clip can’t go, its red outline is now see-through, so the clips already on that track stay in view.

Drums

  • New drum pads start at their kind’s voice count again: a kick cuts itself off, crash and ride pads ring three hits deep, the others two. New Sampler tracks still start at one voice.

Projects and files

  • New: Samples keeps every sample you load, in any project: from Files, videos, recordings, pad loads and Arrangement imports. Its copy takes no extra storage.
  • Browse > Samples now lists all of them, newest first, with All and This Project, and shows a saved setup in bold.
  • Audio you imported or recorded before this update joins Samples the next time its project opens.
  • Settings now opens from the Projects screen too, from the gear at the top.

Everywhere

  • Holding the Device tab brings up the Device Chain sooner.

DAHW v0.51

October 20, 2026 Build 51

Sampler

  • A new Sampler track now plays one voice, so each note cuts off the one before it with a quick fade. Raise Voices on the Sound page to play chords.
  • Projects you already made keep their voice count.

Arrangement and clips

  • New: Drag a clip up or down to move it onto another track, as well as along the timeline. MIDI clips go onto MIDI tracks and audio clips onto audio tracks.
  • While a clip is over another track, it waits dimmed where it was and an outline shows where it will land. When you let go, it replaces what it covers there, just as moving along the timeline does.
  • Where a clip can’t go, the outline turns red with a cross and the display says why. Letting go there leaves everything as it was.
  • In select mode, drag any chosen clip up or down to move them all, each by the same number of tracks.
  • Each move is a single undo step. With VoiceOver, every clip has Move to Track Above and Move to Track Below.
  • New: Legato on the piano roll’s Transform page, in place of Reverse. Each note ends where the next one starts and the last keeps its length. It works on the selected notes, or on every note when none are selected.

Drums

  • New drum pads also play one voice, cymbals included: hitting a pad again fades out its last hit. Raise Voices on the pad’s Sound page to let hits ring over each other. Pads in projects you already made keep their count.

Everywhere

  • The project menu’s Developer section now shows only Performance.

DAHW v0.50

October 17, 2026, early morning Build 50

Sampler

  • The waveform in the record sheet now draws as you record. Before, it could stay still until the take ended.
  • When you import a file or a video, its waveform fills in smoothly as the audio is read, and stops redrawing once the import is done.
  • An import’s waveform fills in as it is read in its Browse row too.
  • In Browse, the playhead now moves across the waveform while a sample previews.

Sound and mix

  • Effects with nothing to play now rest and use almost no processing power: Return A’s Reverb and Return B’s Delay while nothing is sent to them, a silent track’s effects once their tail has faded, and the master Limiter once the song stops.
  • A resting effect wakes on the very first sound, so no note or tail is cut short, and an export sounds exactly as it would without resting.
  • Songs that leave the returns unused now need noticeably less processing power while they play.
  • Complex warp keeps a sound panned to one side even steadier around drum hits when the sample plays much slower or faster than its own tempo. The small dip that was left is now about half as deep.

Projects and files

  • Samples a project no longer uses are now removed when the project opens or closes, freeing storage: old versions left behind by edits, renders, re-rolled drum pads and replaced takes.
  • Anything a Sampler, a drum pad or an audio clip plays is always kept, and so is audio saved with Flatten’s Keep in Samples.
  • Nothing is removed while a project is open, so Undo and Redo always find their audio.
  • Settings > Remove Unused Samples follows the same rule, and now also lists samples a project still holds but no longer plays, by the names the project gives them.

DAHW v0.49

October 17, 2026, early morning Build 49

Sampler

  • Fit to Bars, on the Tempo page: mark a loop on the beat, say how many bars it is, and tap Fit. The sample’s tempo comes from the loop, so the whole sample plays in time with your song, not only the loop.
  • The first Fit puts bar 1 on the loop’s start, shown as an orange 1 flag on the ruler, and moves Start to the first downbeat. Silence or crackle at the beginning of the file is skipped, and a pickup before the downbeat keeps its beat.
  • Fit again over a longer loop, such as 16 bars, to fine-tune: bar 1 stays where it is and only the tempo changes.
  • If Warp is off, Fit turns it on: Complex the first time, then whichever mode you used last.
  • Each Fit is a single undo step, and a short notice says what changed, with an Undo button.
  • On the Tempo page the loop bar shows its length in bars. Tap it to pick 1 beat, 2 beats or 1 to 16 bars, each listed with the tempo it gives, or tap Other to type any count.
  • In Time, a new button on the Sample BPM tile, plays the loop on the song’s bars with only the metronome click, so every change is heard against the beat right away. Tap it again, press Stop, or leave the Sampler to end it; the playhead goes back to where it was.
  • Sample BPM: the minus and plus buttons now step by 0.01, and while you adjust it the display says how far the last bar moves. Tap tempo moved into the Sample BPM control.
  • With Sample BPM open, drag any bar line after bar 1 onto its drum hit: the tempo follows as you drag and bar 1 stays put. The whole drag is one undo step.
  • New Hit snap: markers land on the start of the nearest hit, and the arrow buttons step from hit to hit.
  • Hold the Loop Start handle on the Tempo page for Set Bar 1 Here, Move Start Here and Zoom to Loop.
  • Snap moved into the panel that opens when you select a handle, as on the Loop and Region pages. On the Tempo page, Loop End’s panel adds To End, which moves Loop End to the last bar line.
  • The waveform’s zoom button that shows the whole sample is now called All.
  • Bars before bar 1 are numbered 0 and −1.
  • In landscape, action buttons such as Add Slice now match the height of the controls beside them.

Everywhere

  • At the largest text sizes, a control’s top row keeps its value whole, and notices wrap onto a second line instead of being cut off.
  • Fit, In Time, the bar 1 flag, the loop bar’s lengths and moving a bar line onto its hit all work with VoiceOver.

DAHW v0.48

October 16, 2026, evening Build 48

Sampler

  • With Warp set to Complex, a short reminder under Warp suggests flattening finished parts to audio, since Complex uses the most power.
  • Slice numbers on the waveform no longer touch or get crossed by slice lines at any text size, and a number is never cut off at the end of the waveform. Where slices sit close together, fewer numbers are shown.
  • With Glide on, starting playback in the middle of a note now plays it at the pitch the song has there, instead of sliding in from the last key you played.
  • A Sampler track whose sample finishes loading after playback has started now sounds right away, instead of staying silent until its next note.

Arrangement and clips

  • The playhead in Clip View no longer freezes while the song plays: it moves smoothly in the piano roll, in portrait and landscape, on the ruler’s marker and across a drum pad’s waveform.
  • Consolidate keeps an audio clip’s loop crossfades, so the consolidated clip sounds the same as the original at every loop seam, with no clicks.
  • If the phone falls behind for a moment while you record (an audio dropout), the rest of the take now stays on the beat instead of landing late.

Drums

  • Switching a Drum Rack pad to Complex warp while the beat plays no longer plays its next hit at the wrong pitch.
  • The same holds when you raise a Complex pad’s voices or drop a new sample on a pad set to Complex.

Sound and mix

  • New effect: Auto Pan. It moves a track between left and right, or pulses its volume like a tremolo when Phase is set to 0. Find it in Add Effect, right after Auto Filter.
  • Auto Pan has Amount, Rate (in Hz or synced to the song), Phase, Offset, Wave (sine, triangle, saw up, saw down or random), Shape and Invert. Its page draws what the left and right sides hear, with a playhead that follows the sound.
  • Effects synced to the song, like Auto Pan and Auto Filter’s LFO, now restart exactly at Loop Start when the arrangement loop jumps back, so every pass of the loop sounds the same.
  • Turning on Solo for a muted track or return now unmutes it, and one Undo puts both back. This works from every Solo button: the mixer, the Levels page, the Arrangement’s track headers and the landscape piano roll.
  • Complex warp keeps a sound panned to one side at a steady level around drum hits. Before, it could dip by up to 4 dB right after each hit.

Everywhere

  • Settings > About now links to the DAHW website and the Support page.
  • Projects and racks with damaged or out-of-range effect settings now open safely instead of closing the app.
  • Play DAHW from a hardware keyboard, the way a desktop DAW’s computer MIDI keyboard works: A S D F G H J K L ; ' are the white keys and W E T Y U O P the black keys, played on the selected track. Works with an iPad Magic Keyboard and with a Bluetooth or USB keyboard on iPhone.
  • On a hardware keyboard, Z and X move the octave and C and V change the velocity, with a short readout at the top of the screen. Keys are read by their position, so other keyboard layouts work the same way.
  • Space plays and stops the song, Cmd+Z undoes and Shift+Cmd+Z redoes.
  • Hold the Device tab for half a second to open the selected track’s device chain on top of the page you are on. A quick tap still opens the Device page. With VoiceOver, use the Show Device Chain action on the Device tab.

DAHW v0.47

October 16, 2026, afternoon Build 47

Sampler

  • Fades on the waveform now sit where you hear them, also when a sample is transposed in Re‑Pitch or plays with Warp off.
  • A reversed sample draws its fades at the end it plays from.
  • Starting the song in the middle of a note or a clip picks the sample up exactly where it would be: a Ping-Pong loop runs backwards on its way back, and Repeats count right.
  • Switching Warp to Complex while notes play no longer lets a note slip out in Re‑Pitch.
  • Opening a project keeps each slice’s settings on its own slice.
  • Slice numbers stay apart at large text sizes.

Arrangement and clips

  • Select several clips: choose Select in a clip’s menu, then tap clips or drag a box across empty lanes.
  • Drag any selected clip to move them all; the bar below duplicates, copies, mutes or deletes them together.
  • Copy and paste several clips across tracks: long press an empty bar to see where each one will land.
  • The clip menu has Paste After, and the loop menu has Select Clips in Loop.
  • Piano roll, Select mode: a tap adds or removes a note, a box adds more, and dragging any chosen note moves them all.
  • Piano roll Transform page: Select All, Copy and Cut. Long press an empty spot to paste, in this clip or another.
  • Quantize opens a panel with Quantize To, Amount, Start and End, and shows the result before you apply it.
  • An audio take is drawn in its lane while it records.

Drums

  • The Steps grid marks each hit played early or late with a small tick, and VoiceOver says by how much.
  • Quantize is in the drum clip’s menu, with the result previewed on the steps.
  • Notes pasted into a drum clip stay on their pads.
  • Drag a sample from Browse onto a pad: long press the row, rest on the Device tab, then drop it on a pad.
  • The Kit page’s rows fit at the largest text sizes.

Sound and mix

  • Settings > Audio > Recording Latency: Check measures your headphones or speakers with eight clicks, or set it by hand, so takes land on the beat (useful with AirPods).
  • Input Quantize adds 1/4 and triplets (1/8T, 1/16T), in the transport sheet and the pad view.
  • After an audio hiccup, held sounds stay in time with the rest of the song.
  • The filter’s Velocity and the LFO’s Amount have octave steps.

Projects and files

  • Imports keep going when you switch to another app: files, videos, MIDI files, pads and racks.
  • Autosave never saves an older version of a project over a newer one.
  • Loading two Drum Racks at the same time no longer leaves pads empty.

Everywhere

  • Notices say what was copied or pasted, with Undo whenever there is something to take back.
  • Light mode: the split dividers’ grips and the octave stepper stand out better.
  • The Device Chain’s title wraps at the largest text sizes instead of being cut off.
  • With VoiceOver, switches on tiles now turn on and off with a double tap.

DAHW v0.45

October 16, 2026, midday Build 45

Sampler

  • One Undo now takes back a whole sample load or edit (Crop, Reverse, Normalize, the fades, Render, a new recording), where some took two.
  • The Slices page is simpler: Delete Slice sits beside Add Slice, the slice count shows under Slice By, and Pads From is gone.
  • The arrows beside the slice pads switch between slices 1 to 8 and 9 to 16.
  • Sounds with an uncertain tempo, like a spoken phrase, now load unwarped and play at their own length.
  • Create Clip from Loop keeps the loop’s Ping-Pong and crossfade.

Arrangement and clips

  • Tap a track’s name to open its instrument on Device. Return A, Return B and Master open Mix with their channel in view.
  • Hold Play or Stop for half a second to jump back to the start of the song.
  • On the Clip page, Play from a stop starts at the open clip.
  • A clip’s long-press menu is shorter: Split stays in the bar at the bottom, and Reverse for notes is now on the piano roll’s Transform page.
  • Clip View shows when a clip is muted, with an Unmute button, and dims the notes past the clip’s end.
  • Overdubbing past the end of a looping clip keeps every note you play.
  • An audio clip with Warp off keeps its length in time when you change the tempo.
  • The line under the song’s name counts only your tracks and shows the song’s length.

Drums

  • A recording started from a stop begins on the nearest sixteenth, and the new take opens in Clip View with each hit on its own step.
  • Setting a drum clip’s length on the Steps grid now loops that part, in the song as well.
  • A hit played just before the loop comes round shows on step 1, where you can remove it, and Quantize moves it to the loop’s start.
  • Loading a sound onto a pad from anywhere plays the whole sound, as loading from the Pad page always did.
  • Browse’s Drum Pad button names the pad it will replace.

Sound and mix

  • Every effect page has a menu (the round button with three dots) to duplicate, move or delete the effect, with Undo.
  • Unsolo All: tap the Master’s S to clear every solo at once. It is also on the Arrangement’s Master row and on Master’s Levels page.
  • In Mix, the Levels, Pan and Sends switch sits under the faders, right above its controls.
  • Holding a fader shows its value at the top while every channel’s name stays in view.
  • Analog’s Mod page in landscape shows the filter envelope and the LFO side by side.

Projects and files

  • Delete recordings and imported sounds in Browse. DAHW warns you when a project still uses one.
  • A project saved by a newer version of DAHW now shows in Projects, marked as such, instead of disappearing.
  • A message no longer closes the sheet you have open, such as Export or Settings.
  • Settings > Storage counts your recordings correctly.
  • Project thumbnails sit on a grey backdrop in light mode, as they do in dark.

Everywhere

  • Big songs keep up: dragging clips and moving faders follow your finger without lag.
  • DAHW keeps only the sounds a song plays in memory, so long recordings and undone edits use far less.
  • Larger text sizes: numbers stay whole in tiles, mixer names no longer break, and an empty Sampler fits.
  • Reduce Motion now also stops the drum step grid’s scrolling animation.
  • VoiceOver: Back to Start on Play, Open Device on a track’s name, and clearer slice and solo labels.

DAHW v0.43

October 16, 2026, early morning Build 43

Sampler

  • Slicing is one Undo step, and slices made by beat follow a new tempo.
  • The slice pads and the landscape keys play a sample loaded on any page.
  • Render keeps the right tempo, and Detect no longer offers a doubled one.
  • A sound without a beat loads at its own length instead of being stretched.
  • Short loops show their length instead of 0 bars, and controls you cannot use look disabled.
  • A loop no longer goes silent when Start is moved past it.

Arrangement and clips

  • New: Mute Clip and Unmute Clip in a clip’s More menu.
  • Split no longer clicks at the cut in any warp mode, and splitting while playing keeps the rest of the clip playing.
  • Moving, shortening or transposing a note while it sounds stops it at once instead of letting it ring.
  • Consolidate and Import Audio are one Undo step, and Consolidate keeps an audio clip’s fades.
  • A trimmed audio clip starts without a click.
  • Overdub keeps the track’s other clips playing while it records.

Drums

  • Re-rolling a rack keeps the sounds you loaded yourself.
  • Replace names the pad after its new sound.
  • A new Drums track keeps its kit after an Undo or a relaunch.
  • Two-finger rolls on the pad view play both pads.
  • The pad editor’s arrows skip empty pads.
  • In 7/8 the step grid shows all 14 steps, and it opens on the row you are editing.

Sound and mix

  • Soloing Return A or Return B plays that return on its own, still fed by your sends.
  • An Analog oscillator left out by the Osc Mix says so, with a Mix button.
  • Effect controls show their full names (Sidechain Filter is now HP Filter).
  • Long preset names no longer push the Device screen off the edge.
  • Save as Preset no longer suggests a name you already used.
  • Drums from a single-track MIDI file get a proper name instead of Channel 10.

Projects and files

  • If a save fails, DAHW tells you once, keeps your edits and tries again.
  • A failed export says why on the Export sheet, and very long names export fine.
  • New Project and Delete stay quick with dozens of projects.
  • A sound that finishes loading after you switch projects lands in the right one.
  • A project can no longer be renamed to a blank name.

Everywhere

  • New: with nothing playing, recording or exporting, DAHW pauses its audio in the background to save battery.
  • Leaving a page or the app while the song plays no longer cuts its notes or audio.
  • Calls, Siri, headphones, Control Center and the Home Screen were each checked, and a call now silences every track.
  • The Record button is easier to hit.
  • Long samples load without freezing the screen.
  • Larger text sizes: header pills and titles wrap cleanly.
  • VoiceOver reads each note’s pitch in the piano roll.
