Updates

What changed in each build of DAHW, newest first.

TestFlight lists these builds as version 0.42 with the build number, for example 0.42 (45), while the app shows the same build as DAHW v0.45 on its Projects screen.

DAHW v0.47

October 16, 2026, afternoon Build 47

Sampler

  • Fades on the waveform now sit where you hear them, also when a sample is transposed in Re‑Pitch or plays with Warp off.
  • A reversed sample draws its fades at the end it plays from.
  • Starting the song in the middle of a note or a clip picks the sample up exactly where it would be: a Ping-Pong loop runs backwards on its way back, and Repeats count right.
  • Switching Warp to Complex while notes play no longer lets a note slip out in Re‑Pitch.
  • Opening a project keeps each slice’s settings on its own slice.
  • Slice numbers stay apart at large text sizes.

Arrangement and clips

  • Select several clips: choose Select in a clip’s menu, then tap clips or drag a box across empty lanes.
  • Drag any selected clip to move them all; the bar below duplicates, copies, mutes or deletes them together.
  • Copy and paste several clips across tracks: long press an empty bar to see where each one will land.
  • The clip menu has Paste After, and the loop menu has Select Clips in Loop.
  • Piano roll, Select mode: a tap adds or removes a note, a box adds more, and dragging any chosen note moves them all.
  • Piano roll Transform page: Select All, Copy and Cut. Long press an empty spot to paste, in this clip or another.
  • Quantize opens a panel with Quantize To, Amount, Start and End, and shows the result before you apply it.
  • An audio take is drawn in its lane while it records.

Drums

  • The Steps grid marks each hit played early or late with a small tick, and VoiceOver says by how much.
  • Quantize is in the drum clip’s menu, with the result previewed on the steps.
  • Notes pasted into a drum clip stay on their pads.
  • Drag a sample from Browse onto a pad: long press the row, rest on the Device tab, then drop it on a pad.
  • The Kit page’s rows fit at the largest text sizes.

Sound and mix

  • Settings > Audio > Recording Latency: Check measures your headphones or speakers with eight clicks, or set it by hand, so takes land on the beat (useful with AirPods).
  • Input Quantize adds 1/4 and triplets (1/8T, 1/16T), in the transport sheet and the pad view.
  • After an audio hiccup, held sounds stay in time with the rest of the song.
  • The filter’s Velocity and the LFO’s Amount have octave steps.

Projects and files

  • Imports keep going when you switch to another app: files, videos, MIDI files, pads and racks.
  • Autosave never saves an older version of a project over a newer one.
  • Loading two Drum Racks at the same time no longer leaves pads empty.

Everywhere

  • Notices say what was copied or pasted, with Undo whenever there is something to take back.
  • Light mode: the split dividers’ grips and the octave stepper stand out better.
  • The Device Chain’s title wraps at the largest text sizes instead of being cut off.
  • With VoiceOver, switches on tiles now turn on and off with a double tap.

DAHW v0.45

October 16, 2026, midday Build 45

Sampler

  • One Undo now takes back a whole sample load or edit (Crop, Reverse, Normalize, the fades, Render, a new recording), where some took two.
  • The Slices page is simpler: Delete Slice sits beside Add Slice, the slice count shows under Slice By, and Pads From is gone.
  • The arrows beside the slice pads switch between slices 1 to 8 and 9 to 16.
  • Sounds with an uncertain tempo, like a spoken phrase, now load unwarped and play at their own length.
  • Create Clip from Loop keeps the loop’s Ping-Pong and crossfade.

Arrangement and clips

  • Tap a track’s name to open its instrument on Device. Return A, Return B and Master open Mix with their channel in view.
  • Hold Play or Stop for half a second to jump back to the start of the song.
  • On the Clip page, Play from a stop starts at the open clip.
  • A clip’s long-press menu is shorter: Split stays in the bar at the bottom, and Reverse for notes is now on the piano roll’s Transform page.
  • Clip View shows when a clip is muted, with an Unmute button, and dims the notes past the clip’s end.
  • Overdubbing past the end of a looping clip keeps every note you play.
  • An audio clip with Warp off keeps its length in time when you change the tempo.
  • The line under the song’s name counts only your tracks and shows the song’s length.

Drums

  • A recording started from a stop begins on the nearest sixteenth, and the new take opens in Clip View with each hit on its own step.
  • Setting a drum clip’s length on the Steps grid now loops that part, in the song as well.
  • A hit played just before the loop comes round shows on step 1, where you can remove it, and Quantize moves it to the loop’s start.
  • Loading a sound onto a pad from anywhere plays the whole sound, as loading from the Pad page always did.
  • Browse’s Drum Pad button names the pad it will replace.

Sound and mix

  • Every effect page has a menu (the round button with three dots) to duplicate, move or delete the effect, with Undo.
  • Unsolo All: tap the Master’s S to clear every solo at once. It is also on the Arrangement’s Master row and on Master’s Levels page.
  • In Mix, the Levels, Pan and Sends switch sits under the faders, right above its controls.
  • Holding a fader shows its value at the top while every channel’s name stays in view.
  • Analog’s Mod page in landscape shows the filter envelope and the LFO side by side.

Projects and files

  • Delete recordings and imported sounds in Browse. DAHW warns you when a project still uses one.
  • A project saved by a newer version of DAHW now shows in Projects, marked as such, instead of disappearing.
  • A message no longer closes the sheet you have open, such as Export or Settings.
  • Settings > Storage counts your recordings correctly.
  • Project thumbnails sit on a grey backdrop in light mode, as they do in dark.

Everywhere

  • Big songs keep up: dragging clips and moving faders follow your finger without lag.
  • DAHW keeps only the sounds a song plays in memory, so long recordings and undone edits use far less.
  • Larger text sizes: numbers stay whole in tiles, mixer names no longer break, and an empty Sampler fits.
  • Reduce Motion now also stops the drum step grid’s scrolling animation.
  • VoiceOver: Back to Start on Play, Open Device on a track’s name, and clearer slice and solo labels.

DAHW v0.43

October 16, 2026, early morning Build 43

Sampler

  • Slicing is one Undo step, and slices made by beat follow a new tempo.
  • The slice pads and the landscape keys play a sample loaded on any page.
  • Render keeps the right tempo, and Detect no longer offers a doubled one.
  • A sound without a beat loads at its own length instead of being stretched.
  • Short loops show their length instead of 0 bars, and controls you cannot use look disabled.
  • A loop no longer goes silent when Start is moved past it.

Arrangement and clips

  • New: Mute Clip and Unmute Clip in a clip’s More menu.
  • Split no longer clicks at the cut in any warp mode, and splitting while playing keeps the rest of the clip playing.
  • Moving, shortening or transposing a note while it sounds stops it at once instead of letting it ring.
  • Consolidate and Import Audio are one Undo step, and Consolidate keeps an audio clip’s fades.
  • A trimmed audio clip starts without a click.
  • Overdub keeps the track’s other clips playing while it records.

Drums

  • Re-rolling a rack keeps the sounds you loaded yourself.
  • Replace names the pad after its new sound.
  • A new Drums track keeps its kit after an Undo or a relaunch.
  • Two-finger rolls on the pad view play both pads.
  • The pad editor’s arrows skip empty pads.
  • In 7/8 the step grid shows all 14 steps, and it opens on the row you are editing.

Sound and mix

  • Soloing Return A or Return B plays that return on its own, still fed by your sends.
  • An Analog oscillator left out by the Osc Mix says so, with a Mix button.
  • Effect controls show their full names (Sidechain Filter is now HP Filter).
  • Long preset names no longer push the Device screen off the edge.
  • Save as Preset no longer suggests a name you already used.
  • Drums from a single-track MIDI file get a proper name instead of Channel 10.

Projects and files

  • If a save fails, DAHW tells you once, keeps your edits and tries again.
  • A failed export says why on the Export sheet, and very long names export fine.
  • New Project and Delete stay quick with dozens of projects.
  • A sound that finishes loading after you switch projects lands in the right one.
  • A project can no longer be renamed to a blank name.

Everywhere

  • New: with nothing playing, recording or exporting, DAHW pauses its audio in the background to save battery.
  • Leaving a page or the app while the song plays no longer cuts its notes or audio.
  • Calls, Siri, headphones, Control Center and the Home Screen were each checked, and a call now silences every track.
  • The Record button is easier to hit.
  • Long samples load without freezing the screen.
  • Larger text sizes: header pills and titles wrap cleanly.
  • VoiceOver reads each note’s pitch in the piano roll.
