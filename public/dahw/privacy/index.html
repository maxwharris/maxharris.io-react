DAHW Privacy Policy

DAHW (Digital Audio Handheld Workstation), an app by Max Harris. Effective October 15, 2026.

The short version

DAHW collects no data. Your projects, samples and recordings stay on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and DAHW does not send anything to a server.

What stays on your device

  • Projects, samples and recordings. Everything you make is saved in DAHW's own storage on your iPhone. It leaves the device only when you choose to export or share it, for example by saving a mixdown to Files or sharing a rack file.
  • Microphone. DAHW uses the microphone only while the Record screen is open, to record into the Sampler or onto a track. Recordings are saved on your iPhone and are never uploaded.
  • Photos and Files. When you pick a video or an audio file, DAHW reads only the item you chose, to import its sound. It does not browse or read the rest of your library.
  • Settings. Preferences such as light or dark appearance and the audio buffer size are stored on your iPhone.

Apple services

If you install DAHW through TestFlight or the App Store, Apple handles the download and, if you have turned it on, shares crash reports and diagnostics with developers under Apple's own privacy policy. As a TestFlight tester, Apple may also share the name and email you joined with, and any feedback you send, with the developer. DAHW itself adds nothing to this.

Children

DAHW collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
