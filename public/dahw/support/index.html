Support

Help with DAHW, how to send feedback, and what happens to your data.

Get help

Found a problem or have a question? Email maxwilliamharris@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model, your iOS version and the DAHW version (shown in Settings), and what you were doing when it happened.

Feedback from TestFlight

Testing through TestFlight? You can send feedback with a screenshot, from DAHW itself or from the TestFlight app.

  1. While you use DAHW, take a screenshot, tap its preview, then the share button, and choose Share Beta Feedback.
  2. Or open the TestFlight app, tap DAHW, then Send Beta Feedback.

Your projects

Your projects live only on your iPhone. To keep a copy of a song, export it (whole song or stems) from the Arrangement’s Export sheet and save it to Files.

What it runs on

iPhone with iOS 18 or later. On an iPad, the iPhone version runs.

Privacy

No account. Your music stays on your phone.

  • DAHW collects no data: no analytics, no ads, no tracking, and nothing is sent to a server.
  • The microphone is used only when you record.
  • Photos and Files hand DAHW only the video or file you pick.
